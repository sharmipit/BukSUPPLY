About Us

What is BukSUPPLY?

BukSUPPLY was made specifically for the Property and Supply Management Office (PSMO) of Bukidnon State University to eliminate tedious, paper-based requisition forms, unorganized inventory tracking, and long physical queues at the Main Campus Bodega.

By providing an intuitive digital portal, this system connects university faculty, administrative employees, custodians, and recognized student leaders with the university's central supply repository in real time.

What You Can Do with BukSUPPLY?

Three simple services to help BukSU students, faculty, and staff request supplies, borrow equipment, and keep track of items

Request Supplies

Bond paper, pens, markers, and office supplies

Look up available supplies online and submit a request in seconds. Once approved, you can pick up your items at the supply window using your digital claim slip.

Borrow Equipment

Projectors, laptops, cables, and other school equipment

Reserve projectors, laptops, and other school equipment ahead of time for your classes, thesis defense, or university events. Return items on schedule with ease.

Track & Manage Records

History, inventory tracking, and clearance status

Keep a clear record of all your requests and borrowed items. Staff can manage live inventory, while students and faculty ensure their semester clearance stays clear.
